Paris

Paris, France · 29 August – 1 September 2014 · draft

Paragraph 1

We walked forty kilometres in four days. It was August, and the city was emptying. Neither of us had been to Paris before. We had no expectations to speak of, only a hotel on the Champs-Élysées and a vague intention to see the things people see.

Paragraph 2

The Eiffel Tower up close is not as beautiful as people pretend. It is iron and rivets and a queue. But from the Trocadéro, across the river, it lifts out of the city like something drawn from memory rather than built. We walked up it anyway. There is a glass floor outside the restaurant on the second level that Hanna would not stand on.

Paragraph 3

We pushed ahead in the queue for Notre Dame, which is not the Swedish way of doing things, but it was a long queue and we were learning. Inside the Louvre, Hanna saw her first mummy. The Mona Lisa was not as small as people had warned us. We had been told to lower our expectations and so we were almost surprised by her.

Paragraph 4

On the Pont des Arts we left a padlock among thousands of others. The bridge was so heavy with locks that the railings sagged.

Paragraph 5

Other things we remember. The bouillabaisse that came with scales and bones and tentacles still attached. The jewellery shop near Place Vendôme where you had to pass two locked doors before they would let you in. The rabbits living wild outside the Musée de l'Armée, and the police officer who blew his whistle when we stepped on the grass to photograph them. The pet shop with kittens and puppies in an aquarium, which upset Hanna. The maître d' at Maison Blanche, who let us in to photograph the tower after the restaurant had closed.

Paragraph 6

People had told us Parisians would be rude. They were not. We came back tired and pleased and slightly in love with the city, which is, I suppose, what Paris is for.

Cut: the kiosk-and-chewing-gum line (felt minor), the four-mile walking detail (already in paragraph 1), and the Jean-Marc Garel Ipitonga name (too specific). Should any of the cuts come back?
