About

I’m Anders. I grew up in rural Småland and stayed in school longer than was strictly necessary. I have worked on microlithography in Stockholm, frozen my toes on Kibo, and done research in Los Angeles and Los Alamos.

I travel with Hanna and Hannes — a family of three who move slowly, write things down, and keep the photographs we meant to throw away.

This is where we gather what we see.

The other two

Hanna

A sporty Gothenburger with a past in football and bandy. Has played berimbau in Bahia, tended bar in Barcelona, flipped burgers on Mallorca, rented skis in Andorra, taken a year off in Santiago, studied Italian in Terracina, and owned a house in Bollebygd.

Hannes

A born conversationalist. Energy in bursts. Trains capoeira, studies Portuguese.
